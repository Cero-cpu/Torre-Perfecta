## Inglés (idioma principal)
**Título (máx. 30):** Torre Perfecta
**Descripción corta (máx. 80):** Tap to stack perfect blocks. A simple, addictive one-tap tower game.
**Descripción completa:**
Torre Perfecta is a one-tap stacking game. Drop each block at the right moment and build the tallest tower you can.

How it works
• Tap to drop the moving block.
• Anything that overhangs gets cut off, so your tower gets narrower.
• Land three perfect drops in a row to win back width.

Features
• Simple one-finger controls
• Quick rounds, perfect for short breaks
• 6 color themes to unlock with your best score
• Stats and a daily streak to keep you coming back
• No internet needed to play
• Available in 10 languages: English, Español, Português, हिन्दी, Bahasa Indonesia, Русский, Türkçe, Français, Deutsch, 日本語

How high can you go? Beat your best score!

Contains ads. Optional rewarded ads let you continue a run.
